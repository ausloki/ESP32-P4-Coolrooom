Recommended Settings — Dessert Plums
(Black Amber · Amber Jewel · Tegan Blue)

ESP32-P4 Coolroom Controller · Profile date 2026-08-07 · ~15° Brix starting assumption · Docs: reference/QUICK_START_GUIDE.md §4b · Companion to recommended_settings_2c.html (general 2 °C food)

1. Assumptions

  • Site crop set: Black Amber, Amber Jewel, and Tegan Blue Japanese dessert plums in one shared coolroom.
  • Spelling: catalogues / papers use Amber Jewel (singular; not “Jewels”) and Tegan Blue (not “Teagan”). UC Davis often writes ‘Blackamber’ as one word — trade name here is Black Amber.
  • Target: shared air hold at 0.5 °C (~15° Brix starting assumption), short-to-medium-term chill — not a freezer, not CA long-haul. Published holds for these three cluster near 0 °C (Tegan Blue trials often 0 ± 1 °C, ~90 ± 5% RH).
  • Shared SP / freeze guard: one setpoint for all three — literature does not give distinct flesh freeze points by cultivar name. Freeze risk tracks Brix / maturity (lowest-SSC pack freezes first). Low Temp Alarm Delta 1.5 °C → trip ~−1.0 °C. Size for the lowest-Brix pack; high-SSC Amber Jewel is usually more freeze-tolerant. Black Amber’s well-documented risk is CI when held too warm (~2–8 °C), hence a tight High Δ as well. Confirm freeze point for your packout / Brix before going colder.
  • Control band: asymmetric — compressor ON at SP+Diff, OFF at SP (here ON 1.5 / OFF 0.5 °C).
  • Plant: single compressor; passive / timed defrost (compressor held off; no heater coil). Evaporator fans hardwired continuous — Fan Relay Enabled stays Off.
  • Probes: Probe 1 = coolroom air (RTD CH1), Probe 2 = evaporator (RTD CH2) — fixed roles. Manual calibration offsets only (no “Calibrate Probes Now”).
  • Humidity: monitored, not controlled — no RH setpoint / hysteresis entity. Aim ~90–95% RH with separate humidification if needed. Dew-Point / Frost-Rate need Internal SHT31 fitted.
  • Priority: freeze protection + stable near-0 hold over aggressive energy saving.
Bench note: RS485 (relay / RTD / optional CT) and expansion I2C (SHT31/SHT20) may be disconnected. Enable what is physically fitted; Modbus timeouts and sensor-offline diagnostics remain expected until wired. End-to-end cooling cannot be proven without RS485 + probes.
vs 2 °C food profile: lower setpoint (0.5 vs 2.0), tighter High/Low alarm deltas (2.0 / 1.5 vs 2.5 / 2.0), same humid-room defrost cadence (6 h + smart/dew/frost when sensors online), ntfy recommended On for remote critical alarms. Same compressor protection timers.

4. Intentionally left at factory default (or omitted)

  • Compressor protection timers (3 / 2 min) — OEM-safe; no plum-specific reason to shorten.
  • Fan Relay Off / Hold Door Open Off / Skip-If-Cold Off — same commissioning caution as the 2 °C profile.
  • Ice / smart thresholds (15 °C, 8 °C, dwells) and Post-Defrost Grace 20 min — start from manual defaults; tune from real frost behaviour.
  • Calibration offsets 0.0 — never invent a correction without a reference thermometer.
  • No RH setpoint / humidity hysteresis — entities do not exist; humidity is report-only + dew/frost triggers.
  • No “Calibrate Probes Now” — removed from firmware; use Probe 1/2 Calibration Offsets only.
  • Per-phrase Speak toggles / ntfy priority selects — leave defaults unless site wants quieter speech or different phone urgency.
  • Firmware YAML initial_values — not changed; this sheet is live NVS + documentation only.

5. How to apply or revert

  • From UI: log in on the web dashboard → set the values in the tables above (or Quick Start §4b).
  • Via REST (LAN): POST http://<ip>/number/<Name>/set?value=… and …/switch/<Name>/turn_on|turn_off (empty body + Content-Length: 0).
  • After ≥30 days of SD logs: optional tools/analyse_logs_tune_settings.py — compare to this profile; WA picking window --window picking. See Quick Start §7.
  • Verify persistence: reboot (do not use plain USB esphome upload — that erases NVS). Prefer ./tools/esphome_flash.sh or OTA if flashing.
  • Backup: once happy, Backup All Settings to SD.
  • Full factory defaults: Factory Reset or flash with --erase-settings.
