The target coolroom temperature. The compressor switches ON once the room is half the Compressor Hysteresis value above this, and OFF the same amount below it. Compared live against the Coolroom Temperature (Primary Control) probe — the big number in the center of the gauge.
How far above Setpoint the room can drift, held for 5 minutes straight, before the High Temperature alarm fires. Example: Setpoint 2.0°C + 3.0°C = alarm above 5.0°C. Shows on the centre status (HIGH TEMP) and the 🔔 alarm icon — no pink strip above the gauge.
Same idea as High Alarm Delta, but for the room running too cold — fires the Low Temperature alarm once the room is this many degrees below Setpoint for a full 5 minutes.
The temperature band the compressor cycles within around Setpoint. Smaller = closer to Setpoint but more cycling/wear; larger = less cycling but more drift per cycle. Also sets the threshold for the No-Cooling alarm.

Saves every control parameter (Setpoint, Alarm Deltas, Compressor Hysteresis, defrost timing, alarm thresholds) to backup.json on the SD card, for recovery after a factory reset. Also runs automatically whenever the setpoint changes, not just on click. (Static preview — doesn't call a real device.)
Reads backup.json back off the SD card and applies every saved parameter immediately, overwriting current values. Also runs automatically once at boot if a backup exists — this button is for restoring manually without a reboot. (Static preview — doesn't call a real device.)

Lists the daily temperature log files on the SD card (one per day, plus a running events.csv) — Download saves the selected file, Delete permanently removes it. This preview shows mock filenames; the real dashboard talks to a live /logs endpoint.
